STAY ALERT

Financial safety

Protecting your money starts with protecting your personal information and taking time to verify requests.

Protect your sign-in details

  • Use a unique, strong password for each important account.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication where available.
  • Never share your password, PIN, one-time passcode or recovery codes.
  • Do not approve a sign-in request you did not initiate.

Recognise warning signs

  • Promises of guaranteed or unusually high returns.
  • Urgent messages demanding immediate payment.
  • Unexpected links asking you to sign in or confirm account details.
  • Requests to move money to a “safe account”.

Verify independently

Contact a bank or provider using the official app, website or phone number you already trust. Do not rely on contact details in a suspicious message.

If you think an account is compromised: Contact your financial provider immediately through its official channel, secure the account, and report unauthorised transactions promptly.
