Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how TopSafeB handles information when you visit this website or create an account to use its learning features. TopSafeB is an independent financial education platform. It is not a bank or financial institution.

In brief: If you register, the website uses your name and email address to support your account and may store your lesson-completion progress. TopSafeB does not ask you to provide bank account details, card numbers, PINs or one-time passwords for its learning features.

1. Information you provide

When you create an account, you may provide your name and email address and set a password. The learning area records which of the available lessons you have completed and relevant progress dates. If you contact TopSafeB, the information you include in your message will also be received.

2. How information is used

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To save and display your learning progress.
  • To allow authorised site administration to review account and learning records and maintain the service.
  • To respond to messages and help protect the website from misuse.

3. Service providers and storage

TopSafeB uses Supabase services for account authentication and storage of learning records. Website files are published using GitHub Pages. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and may handle it on systems located in different countries. Their own privacy and security terms also apply.

4. Sharing and access

TopSafeB does not sell personal information. Account and learning records are intended to be accessible only for the operation of the learning service and authorised administration, subject to the technical and legal requirements of the service providers. Information may be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the website and its users.

5. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to help protect account information. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a unique password and never share your password, PIN, one-time password or banking credentials with anyone.

6. Retention and your choices

Account information and learning progress may be retained while an account is active and for as long as reasonably needed for service, security or legal purposes. To ask a question, request access or correction, or ask for account information to be deleted, contact ebubemichael442@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We may need to verify that the request is yours. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it.

7. Children

TopSafeB is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website changes. The latest version and its update date will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email ebubemichael442@gmail.com.
